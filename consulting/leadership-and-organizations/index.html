Applied Human Systems

See the pattern. Change what happens next.

The way people work together shapes what they can accomplish. Authority, roles, relationships, identity, and unspoken expectations influence every conversation, decision, and effort to change.

Applied Human Systems brings these influences into focus. Combining organizational psychology, systems thinking, and deep expertise in group dynamics, I help you recognize patterns that are difficult to see from within—and identify where you can act.

We turn that understanding into practical changes: clearer responsibilities, more productive conversations, better decisions, and more effective ways of working together.

Executive & Team Coaching

Make the decisions. Have the conversations. Lead the change.

Move forward on the leadership challenges that demand your attention.

I help you work through consequential decisions, navigate difficult relationships, and exercise your authority with clarity and purpose. Together, we examine the situation, recognize how you may be caught in recurring patterns, and develop actions that change what happens next.

Team coaching brings this same focus to the group as a whole. We address the habits and dynamics that weaken collaboration and build the team's ability to make decisions, manage differences, and deliver together.

Through coaching, you will:

  • Make clearer decisions amid uncertainty and competing demands.
  • Address conversations you have been postponing or struggling to resolve.
  • Set expectations, delegate responsibility, and hold people accountable.
  • Expand your ability to influence what happens around you.
  • Build the capacity to recognize and work through future challenges.

Organizational Consulting

Break recurring patterns. Build an organization that can deliver.

Resolve the obstacles that keep capable people from producing the results they are working toward.

I help you identify how structures, relationships, and everyday practices combine to sustain organizational problems. We bring the right people into the process, address competing expectations, and translate insight into changes people can put into practice.

Whether you are navigating change, clarifying accountability, improving coordination, or adopting AI, we connect organizational priorities to how work actually gets done.

For AI workflow adoption, we identify valuable applications, redesign processes, establish human judgment and accountability, and build the working practices that turn new tools into useful capability.

Together, we will:

  • Clarify who is responsible, who decides, and how people coordinate.
  • Address bottlenecks, duplicated effort, and gaps between teams.
  • Turn competing priorities into explicit choices and commitments.
  • Translate change initiatives into everyday working practices.
  • Establish ways to assess results, learn, and adjust.

Facilitation

Have the conversations that change what happens next.

Develop strategy, strengthen your team, and reach decisions you can act on—even when the issues are difficult and perspectives are far apart.

I design and facilitate executive meetings, board retreats, strategy sessions, team-building experiences, conflict conversations, and multi-day off-sites. I bring structure, directness, and close attention to what is happening in the room, helping groups address the issues that matter and move toward meaningful outcomes.

Strategy facilitation

Turns competing ideas and priorities into clear choices about direction, focus, and action. We examine assumptions, work through tradeoffs, and establish ownership for what comes next.

Team building

Strengthens trust through shared experience, candid conversation, and practical agreements. Members develop a clearer understanding of one another and stronger ways to communicate, coordinate, and work through differences.

Conflict facilitation

Brings difficult issues into a conversation people can work with. We examine what sustains the disagreement, clarify what needs to be resolved, and develop workable commitments for moving forward.

Your gathering will be designed to deliver:

  • Clear decisions about the questions that brought you together.
  • Strategic priorities connected to action and ownership.
  • Direct engagement with disagreements that impede progress.
  • Stronger working relationships and shared expectations.
  • Concrete commitments and a process for follow-through.
